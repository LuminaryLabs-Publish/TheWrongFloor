# Wrong Floor sound — 0.3.0

The sound design uses a quiet original music-box phrase, mechanical room tone, local breathing, filtered hallway movement, and a short intrusion sting. Audio is decoded and conditioned before a run; all files stay local on every platform.

## Runtime sources

| Runtime cue | Source | Treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Music box | `lift-music-box.wav`, original synthesis | Six authored bell notes over 16 seconds; generated by `scripts/render-music-box.mjs` |
| Heartbeat | `688735__cloud-10__heartbeat-97-bpm.wav` | Seam crossfade, lowpass, gradual pace/level rise after a visible clue |
| Breath | `ScaredBreathing.wav` | Limited duration and high-frequency rolloff |
| Shaky breath | `213293__aderumoro__shaky-breaths-frightened-scared-young-lady-girl-teenager.wav` | Replaces the earlier breath at high pressure |
| Movement | `656055__nayahnaidoo__stepping-on-bones-with-crunching-owi.wav` | Locate actual transient after quiet lead-in, pan and muffle through doors |
| Distant voice | `405643__deleted_user_5620050__somebody-please.mp3` | Once per run after the opening floors, off to the left |
| Intrusion | `Jumpscare.wav` | Locate the actual transient; short sting only on death |
| Scream | `239902__thesubber13__scream-3.ogg` | Brief, filtered death tail |

The seven supplied recordings remain in base64 chunks. Old music-box assets remain as historical source material and are not loaded. The previous `music-box-clean.ogg` measured almost silent; `music-box-new` did not decode, and the older `music-box` was only two seconds long. Source file gain alone was not reliable evidence of audibility.

Conditioning removes DC, applies short one-shot fades, joins loop seams, and caps sample peaks at 0.75. All channels share one normalization gain. Breathing receives bounded gain; quiet source noise is not aggressively amplified. The music ducks as pressure rises; closing doors attenuate and lowpass hallway sound. Delayed/active cues are cancelled when sealing, retrying, or returning to title. The final mix retains compressor headroom and low-frequency filtering.

Mechanical hum, latch, arrival bell, door impact, and escape chime are original synthesis in `src/audio/procedural.mjs`. Volume, softer scares, and pause continue to apply.

## Verification

`tests/audio.test.mjs` checks conditioning, stereo balance, onset alignment, loop seams, headroom, and door attenuation. Browser acceptance decodes every runtime asset, renders a 17-second mix through arrival / approach / death / retry / seal / reset, checks clipping and stale cue cleanup, and writes `sound-mix-preview.wav` plus `audio-review.json`. This is measured evidence; listen on target headphones and speakers before release.

The Floor 30 menu now consumes stage edges through `floor-30/audio.mjs`. Quiet music and machinery are enabled after a gesture; panic and failure cues are excluded. See `docs/expansion-0.3.0.md` for the complete ownership and event map.
