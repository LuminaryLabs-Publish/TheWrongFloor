# Goal

Wrong Floor should become a polished, readable observation-horror descent in which the player learns normal room rules, detects increasingly dangerous deviations, and survives a deterministic four-and-a-half-minute scored run without losing the physical elevator premise.

## Core outcome

```text
Floor 30 — untimed diegetic opening
        ↓
Floor 29 — scored simulation begins
        ↓
29 → ... → 1 → G
        ↓
30 × 9 seconds = 270 active seconds
```

## Success conditions

- Floor 30 feels like part of the world, not a conventional menu
- the Floor 30→29 handoff never consumes scored time early
- room/threat readability supports observation rather than arbitrary guessing
- authored rooms/characters/elevator/audio remain coherent with the slice
- deterministic rules remain testable
- browser and desktop delivery remain reproducible
- accessibility settings preserve the game loop while reducing motion/flash/scare burden
- historical prototype material remains available for comparison without contaminating current authority
- provenance and third-party boundaries remain explicit
