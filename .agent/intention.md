# Intention

## Start inside the horror space

The player should begin inside the Floor 30 elevator rather than at a conventional menu. The physical panel and DESCEND interaction establish the elevator as the game's primary interface.

## Separate opening tension from scored pressure

Floor 30 is intentionally untimed. The scored loop begins only when Floor 29 opens, protecting the exact 270-second simulation contract.

## Teach normal before breaking normal

The deterministic schedule includes normal baselines so threat recognition can depend on observation rather than random punishment. Dangerous stops should violate a learned room expectation or present a readable threat.

## Preserve deterministic game rules

Schedules, floor ordering, door timing, false-alarm rules, scoring and save behavior are executable contracts. Presentation can improve without silently drifting those rules.

## Use history as evidence, not authority

Frozen milestone branches and the imported 0.2 prototype are useful for regression recovery, comparison and provenance. They do not automatically define current design.

## Keep delivery evidence honest

Browser rendering, desktop packaging, deterministic tests and a human full-run review prove different things. Do not merge them into one generic “works” claim.
